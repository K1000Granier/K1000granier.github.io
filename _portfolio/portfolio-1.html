--- title: "Relativistic Firehose Instability" excerpt: "Kinetic simulations of pressure-anisotropy-driven firehose instability in relativistic and trans-relativistic plasmas.
" collection: portfolio --- In this work, we study the firehose instability in expanding relativistic plasmas using kinetic simulations, with the goal of understanding how pressure anisotropy is generated, how transverse magnetic fluctuations grow, and how the classical firehose threshold is modified in relativistic regimes.
